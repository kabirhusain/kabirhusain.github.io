## Research

We study how molecules evolve to "do the right thing": bind the right partner, slot into the right place, catalyse the right reaction. Particular interests include the evolution of (in)compatibility in genetic and physical interactions, molecular error correction, and the geometry of fitness landscapes.

We use both theory and experiments, and are hosted both at the Department of Physics and Astronomy, and the Laboratory for Molecular Cell Biology (LMCB).
